# Project charter

## Mission

Measure the operational and business impact of vulnerability-prioritisation improvements,
alert-fatigue reduction, and human-reviewed incident-mitigation assistance under finite SOC and
remediation capacity.

## Title decision

The official title is **pending supervisor confirmation**. Preserve both candidates until that
decision is recorded:

1. Business framing: *The Business Impact of Alert Fatigue Reduction and Incident Mitigation
   Automation on SOC Operations in Organizational Settings*.
2. Technical working title: *Business-Aware AI-Assisted Vulnerability Prioritization and Incident
   Mitigation using Organizational Context and Synthetic SOC Simulation*.

Official titles retain their submitted spelling. Project documentation otherwise uses British
English.

## Scope

Core scope is E1–E4: public vulnerability intelligence, a fictional synthetic organisation, an
explicit human SOC workflow, transparent prioritisation, scheduling constraints, and robust
simulation comparison. E5 local AI assistance and E6 replayed/authorised honeypot evidence are
optional extensions and disabled by default.

## Success criteria

- Time-aware, source-traceable inputs with no future-information leakage.
- Identical input snapshots and seeds for policy comparisons.
- Explicit human capacity, governance constraints, timestamps, and uncertainty.
- Multiple replications and sensitivity analysis for dissertation results.
- Reproducible manifests, versioned assumptions, tested code, and clear limitations.

## Non-goals

- Reproducing proprietary Qualys scoring without licensed inputs.
- Claiming synthetic time values as measured enterprise performance.
- Deploying an exposed honeypot or using an LLM as ground truth.
- Modelling a named bank or Ricardo's employer.
