# SOC & IT Operations

## Scope

Alert fatigue, vulnerability management, SOC workflow, triage/remediation, human-in-the-loop
automation, operational metrics, and honeypots/deception.

## Candidate searches

- `security operations centre alert fatigue triage workload`
- `vulnerability remediation prioritisation analyst capacity SLA`
- `human in the loop cybersecurity automation incident response`
- `honeypot dynamic exploit evidence vulnerability prioritisation`

## Include

Empirical SOC/vulnerability-management studies, controlled experiments, workflow/capacity models,
relevant standards, and authorised deception research with clear ethics and telemetry limitations.

## Exclude

Claims without denominators, offensive deployment instructions, source-IP-as-identity assumptions,
and studies that compare automation against no realistic baseline process.

## Checklist

- [ ] Capture operational definitions and censoring.
- [ ] Distinguish alert, finding, case, incident, and remediation ticket.
- [ ] Record human baseline, sample, workload, and capacity.
- [ ] Extract validity/ethics limitations.
