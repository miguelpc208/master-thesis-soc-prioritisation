# Cyber Landscape & Trends

## Scope

Vulnerability/exploitation trends, EPSS and KEV, automation and AI in cyber operations, regulation,
and the changing threat landscape.

## Candidate searches

- `(vulnerability OR CVE) AND prioritisation AND exploitation AND longitudinal`
- `EPSS AND (validation OR calibration OR prediction)`
- `known exploited vulnerabilities AND prioritisation`
- `cybersecurity automation AND governance AND human oversight`

## Include

Peer-reviewed research, primary official datasets/standards, systematic reviews, and clearly dated
methodological reports that support constructs, assumptions, or temporal design.

## Exclude

Undated vendor marketing, unverifiable statistics, duplicate reports, non-transparent rankings, and
sources with no relevance to the research questions.

## Checklist

- [ ] Log database, exact query, filters, counts, and access date.
- [ ] Verify DOI/URL and source date.
- [ ] Extract method, dataset, metrics, findings, limitations, and relevance.
- [ ] Mark source-backed facts separately from project assumptions.
