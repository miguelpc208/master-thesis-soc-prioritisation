# SOC & Organizational Structure

## Scope

Business criticality, ownership, team capacity, SLAs, risk appetite/acceptance, governance, and NIST,
ISO, COBIT, ITIL, and DORA context.

## Candidate searches

- `business criticality vulnerability prioritisation organisational context`
- `cyber risk acceptance remediation SLA governance`
- `SOC organisational structure capacity ownership`
- `(NIST OR ISO 27001 OR COBIT OR ITIL OR DORA) AND vulnerability management`

## Include

Primary standards/regulation, peer-reviewed organisational studies, defensible frameworks, and
evidence that can operationalise constraints or governance controls.

## Exclude

Checklist-only summaries with no authoritative source, organisation-specific claims that cannot be
generalised, and unsupported universal thresholds/weights.

- [ ] Separate mandatory requirement, guidance, and project interpretation.
- [ ] Record framework/version/date and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Map organisational variables to simulation parameters.
